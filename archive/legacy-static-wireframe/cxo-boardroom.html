The room where the deal moves

Fifteen of the people you are trying to reach, in a private room, for a discussion you set the agenda for. No competing tracks, no general audience, no queue afterwards.

This is where technical objections are addressed, where enterprise buying committees meet your engineering and executive leaders, and where target accounts turn into active procurement engagements.

Our strongest partners take both: the main stage establishes category authority across 800+ delegates, while the boardroom converts that authority into fifteen direct conversations with priority accounts.

Four things happen in that room

A structured, Chatham House environment built for high-stakes enterprise alignment.

01 Context

You set the context

A senior executive from your organisation delivers the opening address inside the session. This takes place within the boardroom, not on the main stage.

02 Discussion

You moderate

Partner-moderated peer discussion rather than a vendor pitch. The format is tailored to the architectural questions you need answered.

03 Curation

We curate the table

Attendees selected against your preferred target sectors, organisations and seniority levels, drawn from the verified delegate base.

04 Intelligence

You keep the room

Post-event delegate details for every participant: name, designation and organisation.

Built for a particular kind of seller

  • Complex consultative sales: Where decisions involve multi-stakeholder buying committees across infrastructure, security and finance.
  • Account-based marketing (ABM) teams: Focused tightly on a named list of enterprise accounts in Malaysia.
  • Regional teams without local spokespersons: Providing direct access to Malaysian buyers without needing heavy local field presence.
  • High-growth platform vendors: Seeking to displace incumbents by demonstrating technical superiorities directly to engineering heads.

How we build your table

  1. You provide the target list: Specific companies, job titles and priority industry verticals you wish to convene.
  2. We recruit and qualify: Our delegate acquisition team directly invites and validates matching executive decision-makers.
  3. You approve the table: Full visibility of confirmed participants ahead of the summit.
  4. You lead the conversation: 60 to 75 minutes of closed-door executive dialogue on your terms.
