Maintain an unobstructed perimeter of at least 1.0X around the outer bounding box. X is defined by the cap-height of the capital letter "I" in the "AI" wordmark.

Unacceptable Usage ("Brand Don'ts")

  • Never distort aspect ratios: Always scale with locked proportional constraints.
  • Never place dark typography on dark backgrounds: Always invert "Infra" to white on dark canvases.
  • Never alter gradient angles: A-leg must ascend at 60°; I-stem must ascend at 90°.
  • Never add drop shadows, outer glows, or bevels: Maintain crisp, pure vector silhouettes.

All primary and secondary rectangular action elements must utilize 6px (rounded-md, 0.375rem) corner radii to echo architectural data centre precision and avoid toy-like roundness.

Editorial & Typographic Compliance Rules

Strict non-negotiable rules governing punctuation, orthography, capitalisation, and numeric formatting.

COMPLIANCE MANDATORY
Sentence Case Everywhere

All headings, titles, navigation items, buttons, and body copy use sentence case. Never use all-caps for headings or display text.

Approved: "Indonesia has announced far more..."
Prohibited: "INDONESIA HAS ANNOUNCED..."
En Dash (–) with Spaces

Always use the en dash (–) surrounded by spaces for parenthetical thoughts and date ranges. Never use the em dash (—) or hyphens.

Approved: "24–25 November 2026"
Prohibited: "24—25 November" or "24-25"
Brand Name Capitalisation

"AI InfraNext" is always closed up with an internal capital N. Never use "AI Infranext", "AI Infra Next", or "the AI InfraNext".

Approved: "AI InfraNext"
Prohibited: "AI Infranext" / "the AI InfraNext"
Capacity Units Formatting

Capacity units are formatted as "274MW" and "1,699MW" without space and with comma thousands separator. Every figure carries a status label and plain-text source.

Approved: "274MW operational"
Prohibited: "274 MW", "274mw"
Currency Standards

Currency is written as "USD 499" with ISO currency code, not "$499". Business Pass delegate pass is the only published summit price.

Approved: "Business Pass USD 499"
Prohibited: "$499", "USD $499", "$499 USD"
UK English Orthography

UK English spelling throughout all copy: organise, programme, centre, prioritise, analyse, licence. Avoid ampersands (&) in body copy; write "and".

Approved: data centre, programme, organise
Prohibited: data center, program, organize
